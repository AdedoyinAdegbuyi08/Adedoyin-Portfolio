My Works!


Work 1

RouteNG is a front-end web app that helps commuters in Lagos find their way around the city by public transport. Getting around by danfo, BRT and other informal routes can be confusing, especially for newcomers, so RouteNG turns it into a simple search: enter where you are starting and where you are going, and get clear route guidance.


Built as my final year Computer Science project at Caleb University, it is a lightweight single-page application designed to load fast and work smoothly on mobile, where most commuters will use it. The project is live at routeng.name.ng , deployed on Netlify with a custom domain, and demonstrates my skills in front-end development, user-focused design, deployment, and technical documentation.
